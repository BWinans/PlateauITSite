Service Terms & Billing Policies

Travel & Mileage

Zone 1 included. Standard travel is included within 30 miles of Winslow, the primary service area. Extended travel — including Flagstaff (about 50 miles) — is billed at standard portal-to-portal rates.

Payment Terms

Net 15 / invoiced. Standard invoices issued upon project completion with Net 15 terms. Monthly retainer packages are billed on the 1st of each service month.

Hardware & Licensing

Client direct / cost+. Clients purchase equipment directly or via PlateauIT at cost plus a procurement fee. 100% hardware prepayment required prior to equipment orders.

Project Scopes

Written proposals. Detailed written proposals and fixed quotes provided for network cabling runs, office migrations, server setups, and major infrastructure deployments.

Prices and estimates listed are baseline starting rates and are subject to change based upon actual site assessment, scope, and setup requirements.
